# Getting started

The Asset Tracking System (MoA-ATS) follows every Ministry asset from the day it is received, to the person or office that holds it, to the day it is disposed of. Each step is recorded on the statutory forms (Model 19, 22 and 21), approved in two stages, and kept in an audit log.

## Signing in

1. Open the system's web address in your browser.
2. Type your **email or employee ID** and your **password**, then press **Sign In to Asset Tracking Portal**. The eye icon shows or hides the password.
3. If your password is a temporary one, the system asks you to choose your own before anything else. Use at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number. Nobody else will know it.
4. You land on the page that matters most for your role.

> There is no "forgot password" link. Ask your System Administrator to reset it; you will get a temporary password and choose a new one at your next sign-in.

## Finding your way around

1. The **left menu** lists the pages your role can open. Collapse it with the arrow at the top. On a phone, the main pages are along the bottom and everything else is in the account menu.
2. The **top bar** shows the page name and a search box. On a larger screen it also shows today's date (click it to switch between the Ethiopian, E.C., and Gregorian, G.C., calendar) and, for approvers, a bell with requests waiting for you.
3. Your **account card** (bottom of the left menu, or your avatar on a phone) opens Profile, Documentation and Sign out.

## Who can do what

| Role | What you do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Data Encoder** | Records what arrives, and requests issues, transfers, returns and disposals. Cannot approve. |
| **Team Leader** | Checks requests and endorses or rejects them (Stage 1). |
| **Department Head** | Gives the final approval, or rejects (Stage 2). |
| **Manager** | Watches the dashboard and reports. |
| **System Administrator** | Manages users, roles, employees, stores and settings. Cannot work with the stock or approve. |
| **Employee** | Sees the assets assigned to them. |

The person who makes a request can never approve it. Your administrator may adjust roles, so your menu can differ slightly.

## Search

Press **Ctrl+K** (or **/**) and type at least 2 letters to find an asset by name, code, serial number or slip number. Use the arrow keys and Enter, and Esc to close.

## Staying signed in

You stay signed in for 8 hours, then sign in again. If your access is removed, you are signed out straight away. Use **Sign out** on the Profile page when you leave a shared computer.

> **Tip:** the version of the system you are running is shown at the bottom of the left menu and on the Profile page. Quote it when you report a problem.
