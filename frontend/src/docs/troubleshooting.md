# Questions and problems

## Signing in

| What you see | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **The email / employee ID or password is not correct.** | Check both. You can type your employee ID instead of an email. Ask your administrator if you are not sure of your password. |
| **Too many failed sign-in attempts. Try again in N minutes.** | Five wrong tries on one account block it for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again with the right password. |
| **This employee has no system access.** | You are on the staff register but have no role. Ask your System Administrator for one. |
| **This account has been deactivated.** | Contact your System Administrator. |
| **Your session has ended. Please sign in again.** | You were signed in for 8 hours, or your access changed. Sign in again. |
| Choose a new password | Your password was temporary. Pick your own, at least 8 characters with a letter and a number. |

## Forms and requests

| Problem | Reason and fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| The **Submit** button is grey | A required field (*) is empty, or a number is out of range, or a scan is required. The note beside the button says so. |
| I can't find the **Issue**, **Dispose** or **Transfer** button | Your role may not allow it, or the asset is in another status. Issue and Dispose need an asset **in store**; Transfer and Return need an **issued** asset. |
| **Edit** is greyed out | The Team Leader has already endorsed the request. Ask an approver to reject it, then submit it again. |
| An asset says **pending** | A request is open on it. Only one request can be open on an asset at a time. |
| The file will not upload | Use a PDF, PNG, JPEG or WEBP file of up to 10 MB. |
| **A scanned copy is required** | Your administrator has made the scan compulsory. Attach it. |
| I can't approve my own request | By design. Someone else must approve what you submitted. |
| I rejected a request by mistake | A rejection cannot be undone. The requester submits a new one. |

## Seeing and printing

- **A voucher says "NOT YET APPROVED".** It is a draft. Print again after approval to get the signed copy.
- **I can't see a page in the menu.** Your role does not include it. Ask your administrator if you need access.
- **The page looks old after an update.** Reload the page (Ctrl+F5). If you installed the app on your phone or computer, close and open it again.
- **The calendar is wrong.** Click the date at the top to switch between Ethiopian and Gregorian.

## Asking for help

Tell your System Administrator:

1. what you were doing and what the screen said (the exact words help);
2. the asset's tracking code or the slip number;
3. the **version** shown at the bottom of the left menu or on your Profile page.
