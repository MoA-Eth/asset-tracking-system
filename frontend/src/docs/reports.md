# Dashboard, reports and audit log

## Dashboard (Managers and administrators)

The dashboard shows the whole portfolio at a glance.

1. **Total valuation, Total assets, In store, Issued:** the four numbers at the top, with how much is with staff and how much is in store.
2. **Needs attention:** shown only when there is something to do. Click a tile to go there: pending approvals, pending receipts, issues, transfers and disposals, **At-risk assets** (damaged or needing repair) and **In store over 30 days**.
3. **In store over 30 days:** items that should have been issued within a month, with how many days overdue, the units and the value.
4. **Charts:** stock movement (received and issued per month, last 6 months), value by directorate, categories, condition, the highest-value assets, and assets by location.
5. **Recent activity:** the latest actions. **Full log** opens the audit log.

If the dashboard cannot load, press **Retry**.

## Reports

**Reports** is the official asset registry with valuations and movements. It counts approved assets only.

1. Choose a report: **All Assets**, **Received**, **Available (In Store)**, **Issued (In Custody)** or **Transferred and Returned**.
2. Narrow it by search, **time period** (today, this week, this month, a quarter, a year, or a custom date range), **category** and **location**. The charts follow your filters.
3. Read the table: tracking code, item, category, status, received, issued and in-store units, slip number, date (E.C.), custodian or location, and unit cost.
4. Press **Export CSV** for a spreadsheet, **Export PDF** for a landscape A4 official report, or the print button. They include every row that matches your filters, not only the page on screen.

## Audit log (Team Leaders, Department Heads and administrators)

Who did what, and when. Entries are written by the system and cannot be edited or deleted.

1. Search by person, action, slip number or description.
2. Press **Filter** to choose the **activity type** (Receiving, Issuing, Transfers, Disposals, Approvals, Rejections, Users and settings) and the **time period**.
3. Click the arrow on an entry to see the before and after values, where recorded.
4. **Export CSV** or print it for your records.

> **Tip:** to find the history of one asset, search the audit log by its tracking code, or open the asset in **Assets** and read its **Transaction Requests**.
