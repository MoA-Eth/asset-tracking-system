# Approvals

Every receipt, issue, transfer, return and disposal needs two approvals. The **Team Leader** checks it first (**Stage 1**), then the **Department Head** gives the final approval (**Stage 2**). Nothing changes on the asset until Stage 2 is approved.

You cannot act on a request you made yourself, and you act only on the stage your role covers.

## Review a request

1. Open **Approvals**. The tab **Pending My Action** shows what is waiting for you. The bell in the top bar shows the same, and clicking it opens this page.
2. Search, or filter by type (Receiving, Issuing, Return, Transfer, Disposal). Switch between **Table View** and **Cards View**.
3. Press **View** to see the full record. Open the scanned slip with the paperclip link when there is one.
4. Press **Endorse** (Team Leader) or **Authorize** (Department Head).
5. Add remarks if you wish, then confirm with **Endorse (Stage 1)** or **Authorize & Sign-Off**.

## Reject a request

Press **Reject** instead. You **must** give a reason (up to 500 characters). The reason is shown on the asset, so the person who made the request knows what to correct. They can then make a new request.

## Several at once

Tick the boxes of the requests, then use the bar that appears: **Batch Endorse**, **Batch Authorize** or **Batch Reject**. Add a note (required for a rejection) and confirm. You are told if any could not be processed.

## What happens after approval

| Request | Approved | Rejected |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Receipt (Model 19)** | The asset is **In store**. | The receipt stays as **Rejected**. |
| **Issue (Model 22)** | The asset is **Issued** to the recipient. Part of a batch is split off. | It stays in store. |
| **Transfer (Model 21)** | The new holder, directorate and location apply. | Nothing changes. |
| **Return (Model 21)** | The asset is back in store. | It stays with its holder. |
| **Disposal** | The asset is **Disposed**. Part of a batch is split off. | It stays in store. |

## The tabs

**Pending My Action**, **Stage 1 (Team Leader)**, **Stage 2 (Dept Head)**, **Approved History**, **Rejected** and **All Records**, each with a count.

> **Tip:** once endorsed, a request can no longer be edited by the person who made it. If something is wrong, reject it with the reason and ask them to resubmit.
