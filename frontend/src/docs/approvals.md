# Approvals

Every receipt, issue, transfer, return and disposal needs two approvals. The **Team Leader** checks it first (**Stage 1**), then the **Department Head** gives the final approval (**Stage 2**). Nothing changes on the asset until Stage 2 is approved.

You cannot act on a request you made yourself, and you act only on the stage your role covers.

## Review a request

1. Open **Approvals**. The tab **Pending My Action** shows what is waiting for you. The bell in the top bar shows the same, and clicking it opens this page.
2. Search, or filter by type (Receiving, Issuing, Return, Transfer, Disposal). Switch between **Table View** and **Cards View**.
3. Press **View** to see the request as the voucher it belongs to (Model 19, 22 or 21, or the disposal form), the same paper that is printed and signed. Open the scanned slip with the paperclip link when there is one.
4. Press **Endorse** (Team Leader) or **Authorize** (Department Head).
5. Add remarks if you wish, then confirm with **Endorse (Stage 1)** or **Authorize & Sign-Off**.

## Correct a small mistake

A typo (a quantity, a price, a voucher number) doesn't need a rejection. Open the request and press **Edit**: its record opens in the edit form, where you fix it and press **Save changes**. The request stays with you, at the same stage, so you can then endorse or authorize it. The Team Leader can correct a request waiting for endorsement, the Department Head one waiting for authorization; nobody corrects their own request this way. Every correction is recorded in the asset's history and the audit log ("Corrected by the Department Head at Stage 2: quantity 3 → 4").

## Reject a request

Press **Reject** instead. You **must** give a reason (up to 500 characters). The reason is shown on the asset, so the person who made the request knows what to correct. They can then make a new request.

## Several at once

Tick the boxes of the requests, then use the bar that appears: **Batch Endorse**, **Batch Authorize** or **Batch Reject**. Add a note (required for a rejection) and confirm. You are told if any could not be processed.

## What happens after approval

| Request | Approved | Rejected |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Receipt (Model 19)** | The asset is **In store**. | The receipt stays as **Rejected**. |
| **Issue (Model 22)** | The asset is **Issued** to the recipient. Part of a batch is split off as a new record. | It stays in store. |
| **Transfer (Model 21)** | The new holder, directorate and location apply. Part of a batch is split off to the new holder. | Nothing changes. |
| **Return to store (Model 21)** | The asset is back in store. For a partial return, returned units split off as **In store**; remaining units stay with the employee. | It stays with its holder. |
| **Disposal** | The asset is **Disposed**. Part of a batch is split off as disposed. | It stays in store. |

## The tabs

**Pending My Action**, **Stage 1 (Team Leader)**, **Stage 2 (Dept Head)**, **Approved History**, **Rejected** and **All Records**, each with a live count.

> **High-Value Authorizations:** Requests valued above **10,000,000 ETB** are flagged as High-Value and require Stage 2 authorization by the **Chief Director**.
>
> **Tip:** Once endorsed by the Team Leader, a request can no longer be edited by the person who made it. The approver it waits for can correct a small mistake (see above); for anything else, reject it with a reason so the requester can amend and resubmit.
