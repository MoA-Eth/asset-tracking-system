# My assets

If you have the **Employee** role, you sign in to see the assets that have been issued to you, and nothing else. You cannot see anyone else's assets, and you cannot change anything here.

## See what is assigned to you

1. Sign in with your employee ID (or email) and your password. If your password is temporary, choose your own first.
2. **My assets** opens. The top line tells you how many assets are assigned to you.
3. For each asset you see: the item and its code, the **serial number**, the **quantity**, the **condition**, the date it was **assigned**, and its **status**.
4. Press **Refresh** to load the latest.

## See the details

Click a row to open the details: item code, serial number, category, condition, quantity, directorate, where it was issued from, when it was assigned, the **issue slip number** and what it was issued **for**. Press **Esc** to close.

## What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Assigned to you** | It is yours to look after. |
| **Return pending** | A return to the store has been requested and is waiting for approval. |
| **Transfer pending** | A transfer to someone else has been requested and is waiting for approval. |

A pending request also says who it is waiting for: **the Team Leader** or **final approval**.

## If nothing is shown

If you see "Nothing is assigned to you", no asset is issued to you at the moment. When the store issues an asset to you, it appears here.

If you think an asset should be assigned to you, or a detail looks wrong, **contact the store**.

> You are responsible for the assets assigned to you. Tell the store before you give an asset to anyone else, so the transfer is recorded.
