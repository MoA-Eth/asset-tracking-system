# Transfers and returns (Model 21)

Both are for assets that are **Issued**. A **transfer** moves an asset from one holder to another. A **return** brings it back to a store. Nothing changes on the asset until the Department Head approves.

## Transfer an asset

1. On **Assets**, open the **⋮** menu of an **Issued** asset and choose **Transfer (Model 21)**.
2. Enter the **Model 21 No.** and the **Book**. The **Transfer date** is today; change it if the transfer happened on another day (it can't be in the future).
3. Check the asset. Optionally enter the **accumulated depreciation** (the **chassis number** is under Vehicle & machinery details).
4. Choose **To employee** (required). The **directorate** fills in from the employee; change it if needed.
5. Vehicles and machinery have optional details: plate, engine, accessories, tires and defects.
6. **Recorded by (store man)** is a store man of the store the asset came from, chosen for you when there is only one. His name is printed on the voucher as **Recorded By**.
7. Press **Submit transfer for approval**. The Model 21 voucher opens for printing.

## Return an asset to store

1. Open the **⋮** menu of an **Issued** asset and choose **Return to store (Model 21)**.
2. Enter the **Model 21 No.**, the **Book** and the **Return date**.
3. Choose the **Condition on return** (Good, Fair, Needs repair or Damaged) and enter the **Reason for return**. Note any defects or missing parts.
4. The asset returns to the store it came from. **Received by (store man)** is a store man of that store, chosen for you when there is only one. Attach the **scanned return slip**. If a store man is compulsory and the store has none, it cannot take items back.
5. Press **Submit return for approval**.

## Good to know

- You can **edit** a pending transfer or return until the Team Leader endorses it.
- A request on an asset blocks a second one: only one open request per record.
- **Approved transfer:** the new holder and directorate apply. **Approved return:** the asset is **In store** and no one holds it. **Rejected:** nothing changes.
- A **transfer** has no scan upload.
- Assets issued to an outside organization cannot be transferred or returned yet.
